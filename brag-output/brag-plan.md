# Nostos — Your life, stamped.

## Nine-question rubric
1. Nostos collects visits to real New York places as personal passport stamps with photos, captions and optional songs.
2. Strongest claim/line: “Your life, stamped.” No growth, launch or performance claims.
3. Hook: monumental editorial serif over warm paper, Greek eye collage, electric-blue seal.
4. Source visual: current ivory/blue design references Map and New Stamp; native source confirms place selection, camera capture, caption, stamp creation, passport page flip and landing.
5. Shortest satisfying film: 20 seconds, five linked beats.
6. Polished; Greek cultural/editorial travel journal, not corporate feature tiles.
7. Original warm 96 BPM struck-tine instrumental, soft page/card sounds, one precise stamp impact; no voiceover.
8. Caption: “Your NYC places. Your personal passport. Your life, stamped. Nostos is coming soon to iPhone.”
9. Flow: find a real place → capture a memory → save it as a passport stamp.
