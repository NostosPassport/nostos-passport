# Real-iPhone checklist — unchecked

1. Install SDK 57 compatible Expo Go. Mac and iPhone same Wi-Fi. Scan NOSTOS-IPHONE-QR.png; accept Local Network access. In Safari visit http://10.19.108.26:3001/api/health; expect ok:true and db:up. Sign in to confirm authenticated app/backend connection.
2. Open Passport. Real 3D cover must appear without red screen; drag rotates and does not open; a short tap opens the reader. If GL fails, record device/OS/error; fallback Open stamp pages is available.
3. Reader fills available safe-area space. Tabs/header/counts/Previous/Next/Index controls absent. Close target at least 44pt; Close/reopen retains committed spread.
4. First spread shows inside cover AND bearer face. Left tap remains on first spread. Right blank-page tap advances one spread. Repeated taps during animation do not skip. Drag does not also activate a tap.
5. With four stamps (three page 1, fourth page 2), inspect both final faces. Tap fourth stamp: correct details open, no accidental flip. Return restores same spread. Right final-page tap does nothing. Check every populated page in a larger book.
6. Repeat first/final faces, navigation and Close/reopen with iOS Reduce Motion enabled; no spring turn, no missing face. Close during a turn and reopen: no stuck animation or stale handler.
7. Visit a place and capture a photo using the in-app camera. Check permission granted/denied handling. Create a stamp with actual photo/caption or song; confirm save, display and later reopening; confirm backend connection after reopening app. Do not overwrite existing data or invent a successful test.
8. Background/foreground, rotate device and return from details. Confirm model is responsive and no native GPU crash. Record screenshot/video and device/OS for failures.

The reader uses existing animated app pages, not an openable Blender mesh. That requires a rigged/page-surface export.
