# Design

Nostos is a passport: stamps, places, a quiet and personal feel. The design layer (phase 5 in NOSTOS-BUILD-PROMPT.md)
is a separate pass after the product works with neutral components.

## Principles
- Theme tokens only (`mobile/src/theme`). No raw colors or sizes in screens. SF Symbols for icons. No emoji.
- Nostos's own colors, icon set, name and logo. Never copy another app's logo, icons, exact palette, name or assets.
- Patterns (navigation model, layout structure, gestures) may follow a chosen reference app. Assets may not.
- A stamp is only shown with its author's full profile. Nothing is ever labeled "Anonymous".
- The feed is strictly chronological. No ranking, no personalization, no "for you".
